[i]95 hand-picked mods for a grounded, historical Crusader Kings III. The full load order is included as a ready-made playset file, conflicts are patched, and everything is tested together on 1.20.[/i]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/josheyer-historical-collection/main/images/header_install.png[/img]
[olist]
[*][b]Subscribe to all[/b] with the green button above, and let Steam finish downloading.
[*]Download the playset file: [url=https://github.com/Josheyerr/josheyer-historical-collection/blob/main/playset/Josheyer_Historical.json]Josheyer_Historical.json[/url] (use the download button on that page).
[*]In the Paradox launcher, open [b]Playsets[/b], then [b]Import playset[/b], and choose the file. Every mod lands in the right order, with the right ones enabled.
[*]Start a [b]new game[/b]. This setup is not save-compatible.
[/olist]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/josheyer-historical-collection/main/images/header_load.png[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Unofficial Patch[/b] loads first.
[*]Gameplay mods follow, then culture and religion, then portraits, graphics and interface.
[*][b]Holy Deposition[/b] loads second to last.
[*][b]Josheyer Historical Modpack Fixes[/b] loads [b]last[/b], always. It repairs title creation, hairstyles and leftover culture conflicts between the mods above.
[/list]

[list]
[*]Supported version: [b]1.20.*[/b].
[*]Holy Deposition's war needs the [i]Roads to Power[/i] DLC. Everything else runs without it.
[*]Cultures Expanded and its Renovatio patch are left out, with RICE, Africa Plus, More Holding Graphics and a few older patches. The fixes mod keeps Africa Plus culture data and covers leftover RICE cultures.
[*]If you add your own mods, keep them above the fixes mod.
[*]Bug reports and support: [url=https://discord.gg/rwdGCanWpA]Discord[/url].
